NASA data and resources to plug in

  • JPL Mars Trek: web portal for layering Mars data. Its tools include slope mapping, boulder detection and traverse planning, and data products can be downloaded. This is the main source to replace the generated terrain.
  • USGS Geologic Map of Mars (SIM 3292) and the human-scale Candor Chasma map (SIM 3309) from the USGS Astrogeology Science Center: standardized geologic units, and a model for field-scale mapping.
  • Curiosity route map of its hardest climb (2023): HiRISE-based route that veered 150 m off course to find a slope under 15°. This is the reason for the 15° rover limit in this demo.
  • NASA Worldview: model for a satellite-view, time-aware layer browser.
  • Perseverance Raw Images: over 1 million surface photos for point-of-view previews.

About this demo: the terrain, temperature, radiation and mineral grids on this page are generated procedurally to show the interface and routing logic. Real HiRISE/CTX elevation, THEMIS thermal, CRISM spectral and RAD dose data from the resources above replace the generators in buildTerrain().
